TRAVEL SMART

Helpful travel tips

🗓️ When to visit

Weather varies across Sri Lanka. The southwest and east coast often have different rainy seasons, so check conditions for your exact destination.

🚆 Getting around

Trains, buses, taxis and private drivers are common options. Verify schedules and fares with operators before travel.

🛂 Before you fly

Check official entry requirements, passport validity, travel advisories, and visa rules for your nationality before booking.
